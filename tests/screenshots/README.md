Real phone screenshots for testing the skill and its scripts. The phone status bar, the gesture bar and a system edge-panel handle were removed, so every pixel belongs to the app.

| Folder | App | Screens |
|---|---|---|
| `nova-survivor/` | Nova Survivor (Android, synthwave survivor shooter) | `01-main-menu`, `02-shop-upgrades` |
| `word-wheel/` | Word Wheel (Android, swipe-to-spell word puzzle) | `01-daily-reward`, `02-home`, `03-challenges`, `04-packs` |

Capture details:
- Size: 1189 px wide. Nova Survivor shots are 2576 px tall (the game runs full screen, nothing removed). Word Wheel shots are 2425 px tall (rows 0–109 and 2535–2575 removed).
- The banner at the bottom of the Nova Survivor shots is the app's own AdMob test ad, not a system bar. It is kept on purpose.
